FOR TEACHERS

Your live word list.

This game reads your Google Sheet when the page opens. Add or edit words there, then refresh the game to see the changes.

  1. 01
    Keep your current layout

    Put each new date on its own row in column A. Below it, use B for word, C for part of speech, D for definition, E for synonyms or antonyms, and F for examples. A word appears once it has a definition.

  2. 02
    Allow viewing

    In Google Sheets, choose Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer. Use only vocabulary content you want students to see.

  3. 03
    Use this Sheet

    The supplied Sheet is already connected. You can paste another tab or spreadsheet link below if you want to change the source later.

Your student link is ready

Send this link to your students. When you edit the Sheet, they will see the updated cards when they refresh the page.
